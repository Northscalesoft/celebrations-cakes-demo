Baked with love, from Singapore to Fremont

Celebrations Cakes n Desserts began in Singapore in 2014, out of a love of baking and a passion for art and creativity. Today we bake from our home kitchen in Fremont, CA — still the same way: one cake at a time, completely customised to your theme, so your celebration is memorable for years to come.

Our cakes are known for being soft, moist and balanced in sweetness — made to be enjoyed by kids and adults alike. Eggless and nut-free options are available on request.

Signature Flavours

Our most-loved flavours — each one available eggless, and in most cases nut-free too.

  • Rasmalai Cake

    Soft sponge layered with cardamom-kissed cream and saffron milk — our Indian-inspired signature.

  • Mango Mousse

    Light mango mousse over soft sponge, finished with fresh mango. A summer favourite, all year round.

  • Eggless Cake

    The same soft crumb, no eggs. Available in most of our flavours — just ask when you order.

  • Chocolate Ganache

    Rich dark chocolate layers under silky ganache. For the serious chocolate lovers.

  • Gulab Jamun Cake

    Our fusion favourite — gulab jamun folded into soft sponge with a hint of rose and cardamom.

  • And more…

    Classic vanilla, chocolate, red velvet and seasonal specials — ask us about your favourite flavour.
